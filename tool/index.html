SludgeRoutes Planner: which sludge intervention first, and what is it worth?

Describe a large wastewater plant as its operator knows it. The page runs the sludgeroutes model (the one behind the paper Where sewage sludge goes matters more than how it is treated) in your browser and ranks four interventions against the plant's current practice: diverting sludge from landfill to farmland, capturing landfill gas, digestion and pyrolysis. Mark what you do not know, and it tells you how much the answer depends on it.

SludgeRoutes Planner runs sludgeroutes v1.0.0, the model released with the paper (MIT licence), as a line-for-line JavaScript port checked against the Python on 2,503 test values. It is a screening tool with the paper's assumptions and ranges. It does not replace a site assessment, and it says nothing about what a regulator will permit. Inputs you mark as unknown are drawn across their range together with the model parameters (200 draws), which gives the share of draws in which each choice is best and the expected value of learning each fact before deciding.
